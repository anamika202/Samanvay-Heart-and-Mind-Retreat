## 🍃 Overview

**Samanvay (समनवय)** is an interactive mindful sanctuary created to help untangle emotional fatigue, release accumulated hurt, and anchor awareness in quiet stillness. 

Unlike conventional games centered around scores, anxiety, or compulsive loops, *Samanvay* provides an unhurried, gentle stroll across 24 meditative sanctuaries. Each space invites you to pause, breathe, reflect, and cleanse the heart.

---

## ✨ Features & Architecture

- **🎨 Watercolor Aesthetics & Glassmorphism:** Designed with gentle sage greens, earth tones, and calming visual cues that soothe the eyes.
- **🔔 Procedural Web Audio (100% Offline):** Procedural synthesis of 432 Hz singing bowl resonance, 528 Hz transformation chimes, and gentle meditative soundscapes without external audio dependencies.
- **🧘 24 Mindful Sanctuaries:**
  - **Thodi Saans:** Real-time guided circular breathing visualizer (Inhale 4s, Hold 2s, Exhale 4s).
  - **Apne Saath:** Heart-centered contemplations on self-forgiveness, clarity, and rising above betrayal.
  - **Nirmalta (Cleaning):** Visualization practices inspired by Heartfulness cleaning to consciously release pain and heavy impressions from the back.
  - **Shukriya:** A private gratitude space where reflections dissolve into blooming lotus petals.
  - **Prakriti:** Grounding nature reflections reminding us of our deep, immovable inner roots.
  - **Shanti:** Complete circle of peace and renewed resilience.
